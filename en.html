Enterprise Operating & Innovation Cycle

From your ZIP file to your first research report.

Use quarterly financials and earnings calls to assess innovation acceleration, slowdown, monetization delays and recovery. Revenue growth and attractive margins alone do not prove innovation success.

Start installation ↗

Austin provides the Skills installation files in the Discord group. No downloads are provided on this page.

01

What you get

  • The stage, direction and speed of overall operations and each innovation path.
  • Quarterly revenue growth, gross-margin changes, operating margins, free cash flow and seasonal comparisons.
  • Recurring management KPIs and breaks in the investment → adoption → monetization → profit → cash flow chain.
  • Earliest warnings, confirmation quarters, strongest counterevidence and observable revision conditions.
  • Reinvestment-rate and ROIC calculations, formulas and definitions, with explicit data limitations.

Valuation, share-price forecasts and trading advice are not added automatically.

05

Activate the skill

After installation, enter EOIC in the conversation. It is a skill activation phrase, not a terminal command.

EOIC

Aliases: enterprise operating cycle, enterprise innovation cycle, enterprise operating and innovation cycle. EOIC and English aliases are case-insensitive.

Enter a US ticker and quarter count (4–6 recommended), or a start/end date. For example: WING 6 quarters / WING 2022 to 2024/6.

Reply with SNPS 5 quarters, or use a complete prompt below. 4–6 quarters is a recommendation, not a limit. Missing periods are requested; “use the default” selects six quarters.

Explicit language requests take priority; otherwise the main input language is used. EOIC-only or ticker-only requests follow the conversation language, with Traditional Chinese as the final fallback. Non-English terms include the English name or abbreviation only on first mention.

General research

EOIC Analyze SNPS’s latest five reported quarters in English.

Innovation focus

EOIC Analyze Visa’s latest eight reported quarters and assess whether innovation has accelerated or shifted.

Historical research

EOIC Analyze WING’s latest six reported quarters as of 2026-09-30.

Save to Desktop

EOIC Analyze SFM’s latest four reported quarters and save a Markdown report to my Desktop.

Date ranges select fiscal quarters; “as of” dates limit the publicly available information. Without a cutoff, analysis uses information available on the execution date.
